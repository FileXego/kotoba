# Keep the public courtyard and add lightweight following

Kotoba keeps a chronological public garden and adds following only as a personal reading lens; reciprocal follows dynamically form acquaintance, while reposts, private messages, recommendation ranking, and follower competition remain outside the first social scope. This was chosen over a following-first network and an invite-only topology because it preserves serendipity without making amplification or popularity the organizing principle.

## Consequences

Content policy must distinguish audience from distribution, relationship changes may revoke access immediately, and social quality is measured through conversation rather than reach.
